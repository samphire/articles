=== easy-medium-01 | A Day at the Zoo
On Saturday, my class went to the zoo. We took a bus from the school, and the trip was about an hour long. When we arrived, our teacher gave each of us a map.

First, we went to see the elephants. There was a baby elephant with its mother. The baby was playing in the water and splashing everyone. Next, we visited the monkeys. One monkey took a hat from a boy and ran up a tree. Everybody laughed, but the boy was not happy.

At noon, we had lunch in a small park inside the zoo. I ate a sandwich and an orange. After lunch, we saw a tiger sleeping under a tree. It was the biggest cat I have ever seen.

In the afternoon, we bought a postcard at the gift shop. On the bus home, I fell asleep. It was a long day, but it was a lot of fun.

=== easy-medium-02 | My Grandmother's Garden
My grandmother lives in a small house in the country. Behind the house, there is a beautiful garden. She works in the garden every morning, even in the winter.

In the spring, she plants a lot of vegetables. She grows potatoes, carrots, and an unusual kind of purple cabbage. There is also an apple tree in the corner of the garden. In the autumn, the tree is full of red apples, and we pick them together.

When I visit her, she gives me a small basket. I walk around the garden and collect tomatoes and beans. Then we cook dinner in the kitchen with the vegetables from the garden. The food always tastes better than the food from the supermarket.

My grandmother says that a garden is like a friend. You have to visit it often and take care of it. I want to have a garden like hers one day.

=== easy-medium-03 | The School Festival
Every year, our school has a festival in October. It is the most exciting day of the year for the students.

This year, my class had a food stall. We sold rice cakes and hot chocolate. My friend Minho wore a chef's hat and an apron. He shouted, "Delicious rice cakes! Only a thousand won!" A lot of people came to our stall, and we sold everything before lunch.

In the afternoon, there was a talent show in the gym. A girl from the third grade sang a song, and a boy played the guitar. Our music teacher did a magic trick with a rabbit. The audience cheered loudly.

At the end of the day, the principal gave a prize to the best class. We didn't win, but we got a certificate for the best food stall. We were very proud. I can't wait for the festival next year.

=== easy-medium-04 | Learning to Swim
When I was seven, I couldn't swim. I was afraid of the water. My mother decided that I needed lessons, so she took me to the swimming pool near our apartment.

The teacher was a tall man with a loud voice, but he was very patient. In the first lesson, I only sat at the side of the pool with my feet in the water. In the second lesson, I held a board and kicked my legs. The water went in my nose, and I cried a little.

After a month, I could swim from one side of the pool to the other. The teacher gave me a sticker with a picture of a fish on it. I put the sticker on the door of my room.

Now I am fifteen, and swimming is my favourite sport. I go to the pool three times a week. I am not afraid of the water anymore.

=== easy-medium-05 | A Birthday Surprise

In the morning, my father took her to the market. While they were out, my brother and I cleaned the house. Then we made a cake. It was not a perfect cake. The top was a little burnt, and the cream was too soft. But we put strawberries on it, and it looked nice.

We also made a card. My brother drew a picture of the family, and I wrote a short poem inside.

When my mother came home, we turned off the lights. Then we sang "Happy Birthday" and brought out the cake with a candle on top. My mother was surprised. She laughed and said it was the best cake in the world. I don't think that is true, but it was a very happy evening.

=== easy-medium-06 | The Weekend Market
Every Sunday, there is a market in the square near my house. People come from all over the city to buy fresh food and other things.

The market opens at eight o'clock. When I arrive, the farmers are already selling fruit and vegetables. There is an old woman who sells honey. She always gives me a small spoon of honey to taste. There is also a man who sells fish. His fish is fresh because he catches it in the morning.

My favourite stall sells second-hand books. I usually spend an hour there. Last week I found a book about the history of the city for only two thousand won.

At the end of the market, I buy a bag of oranges and a bunch of flowers for my mother. Then I walk home slowly. The market is the best part of my week.

=== easy-medium-07 | A Snowy Morning
When I woke up this morning, the room was very cold. I looked out of the window, and everything was white. It snowed a lot in the night.

I put on a warm coat, a hat, and a pair of gloves. Then I went outside with my little brother. The snow was deep, and our boots made a funny sound. We made a snowman in the garden. My brother gave it a carrot for a nose and two stones for eyes. I put an old scarf around its neck.

Then our neighbour came out of his house. He had a big shovel, and he cleaned the snow from the road. We helped him for a while. After that, we had a snowball fight with the children next door.

When we came back inside, our hands were red. My mother made us a cup of hot chocolate. It was a perfect winter morning.

=== easy-medium-09 | Camping by the Lake
In August, my family went camping by a lake in the mountains. We drove for three hours to get there.

When we arrived, my father and I put up the tent. It was not easy, and it took a long time. My mother and sister collected wood for a fire. In the evening, we cooked sausages over the fire and ate them with bread.

After dinner, it was very dark. There were no lights, so we could see a lot of stars in the sky. My father showed us the moon through a small telescope. Then we heard an owl in a tree near the tent. My sister was scared, but I thought it was exciting.

In the morning, we went swimming in the lake. The water was clear and cold. It was the best holiday we had this year.

=== easy-medium-10 | A Visit to the Doctor

The waiting room was full of people. A baby was crying, and an old man was reading a newspaper. We waited for about half an hour. Then a nurse called my name.

The doctor was a friendly woman. She looked at my throat and listened to my chest. She said it was only a cold, and I needed to rest. She gave me a prescription for some medicine.

After the visit, we went to the pharmacy next to the hospital. The pharmacist gave me the medicine and told me to take it after every meal. When we got home, I went to bed and slept for the whole afternoon. The next day, I felt a lot better.

=== easy-long-01 | Moving to a New City
Last year, my father got a new job in Daejeon, so my family moved there from Seoul. At first, I was not happy about it. I didn't want to leave my friends, my school, or the little park near our apartment.

The day we moved was very busy. A big truck came in the morning, and two men carried our furniture down the stairs. My mother packed the kitchen things in a box, and I packed my books and clothes. It took the whole day to drive to the new city, and we arrived late at night.

Our new home is an apartment on the tenth floor. From the window, I can see a river and a mountain. My room is bigger than my old room, and it has a desk by the window.

The first day at my new school was difficult. I didn't know anyone, and I sat alone at lunch. But on the second day, a boy named Jisu asked me to play soccer with his friends. He is now my best friend. We go to the same English academy, and we often ride our bikes along the river after class.

I still miss Seoul sometimes. I talk to my old friends on the phone, and in the summer one of them came to visit me. We went to a science museum and ate at a restaurant that sells the best noodles in the city.

Now, after a year, I like my new life. The city is quieter than Seoul, and the air is cleaner. I learned that a change can be scary, but it can also be a good thing.

=== easy-long-02 | The Little Restaurant
My uncle has a small restaurant in the centre of town. It is not a famous restaurant, but the people in the neighbourhood love it. He opened it ten years ago, and he works there every day except Monday.

The restaurant has only six tables. On the wall, there is a painting of the sea and a clock that is always five minutes slow. In the corner, there is an old radio that plays music all day.

My uncle makes the food himself. His most popular dish is a spicy chicken soup. He uses a recipe from his mother, and he says it is a secret. Many customers come every week just for the soup. He also makes an excellent kimchi pancake.

During the summer holiday, I helped my uncle in the restaurant. I washed the dishes, cleaned the tables, and carried the food to the customers. It was hard work. My feet hurt at the end of the day, and once I dropped a plate on the floor. My uncle only laughed and gave me a broom.

I learned a lot that summer. I learned how to cut vegetables quickly and how to talk to customers politely. I also learned that running a restaurant is not easy. My uncle gets up at five o'clock every morning to buy fish and vegetables at the market. He goes home at ten o'clock at night.

At the end of the summer, my uncle gave me an envelope with some money in it. He also gave me a card that said, "Thank you for your help. You are always welcome in my kitchen." I keep the card in a drawer in my desk. One day, I want to have a restaurant of my own.

=== easy-long-03 | The Robot Competition
This year, our science club entered a robot competition. There were four of us in the team: me, Hana, Junho, and Yuri. Our teacher, Ms. Kim, helped us after school.

The rules were simple. Each team had to build a robot that could carry a ball from one side of a table to the other. The robot that did it in the shortest time was the winner.

We worked on the robot for two months. Junho was good at building, so he made the body of the robot. Hana and I wrote the program on a laptop. Yuri designed a small arm to hold the ball. We had a lot of problems. The robot often turned in the wrong direction, and once the arm fell off. Every time something went wrong, we stayed late and tried again.

The competition was in a big hall at the university. There were thirty teams from all over the country. Some of the robots looked very expensive. When we saw them, we felt nervous.

In the first round, our robot dropped the ball. We had only one more chance. Before the second round, Hana changed a number in the program. This time, the robot picked up the ball and carried it across the table in eleven seconds. The audience clapped loudly.

We didn't win the competition. A team from Busan won with a time of eight seconds. But we finished in fifth place, and the judges gave us a special prize for teamwork. Ms. Kim took a photo of us with the prize, and the photo is now on the wall of our classroom.

Next year, we want to build an even better robot.

=== easy-long-04 | A Trip to Jeju Island
In the spring, my family took a trip to Jeju Island. It was my first time on an airplane, so I was very excited. The flight from Seoul was only an hour long. From the window, I could see the sea and some small islands.

When we arrived, my father rented a car at the airport. We drove to a hotel near the beach. Our room had a balcony, and in the morning we could hear the waves.

On the first day, we climbed a mountain called Seongsan Ilchulbong. The path was steep, and it took about thirty minutes to reach the top. My little brother complained the whole way, but the view was amazing. We could see the ocean on every side.

On the second day, we visited a farm where they grow tangerines. The farmer gave each of us a basket, and we picked the fruit from the trees. The tangerines were small and very sweet. We also bought a box to take home to our grandparents.

In the evening, we went to a market and tried a lot of local food. My favourite was the grilled black pork. My mother liked the seafood, and my brother ate three pieces of a tangerine cake.

On the last day, it rained, so we went to a museum. It was a museum about the women divers of Jeju, who are called haenyeo. They dive into the cold sea without any air tanks to catch shellfish. I was surprised that some of the divers are more than seventy years old.

The trip was short, but it was one of the best holidays I have ever had. I hope we can go back to the island again soon.

=== easy-long-05 | The Old Photograph
One day, while I was cleaning my room, I found an old photograph in a box under the bed. It was a black and white photo of a young man standing next to a bicycle. Behind him was a small shop with a sign above the door.

I didn't know the man, so I showed the photo to my mother. She smiled and said, "That is your grandfather. He was about twenty years old in that picture." I was very surprised. My grandfather is now an old man with white hair, and I couldn't believe it was the same person.

That weekend, I visited my grandfather and took the photo with me. When he saw it, his eyes became bright. He told me a long story about it.

When he was young, he worked in a shop that repaired bicycles. The owner of the shop was a kind man who taught him everything. My grandfather rode the bicycle in the photo to work every day, even in the snow. He saved his money for three years and bought the bicycle himself. It was the most expensive thing he had.

He also told me that he met my grandmother because of that bicycle. One day, her bicycle had a flat tyre, and she brought it to the shop. My grandfather fixed it, and he didn't ask her for any money. A week later, she came back with a box of cookies to say thank you.

I asked my grandfather if he still had the bicycle. He laughed and said no, it was lost a long time ago. But he still has the photograph now, because I gave it to him. He put it in a frame on the table next to his bed.

=== easy-long-06 | Volunteering at an Animal Shelter
Last winter, I started volunteering at an animal shelter. The shelter is on the edge of the city, and it takes care of dogs and cats that don't have a home. I go there every Saturday morning with my friend Eunji.

When we arrive, we put on an apron and a pair of gloves. First, we clean the cages and give the animals fresh water. Then we feed them. The dogs are always very excited at breakfast time. They jump and bark until we put the food in their bowls.

After that, we take the dogs for a walk. There is a path along a river near the shelter. Some dogs walk slowly, but others pull hard on the lead. Eunji's favourite dog is a big brown dog named Bori. He is very strong, but he is also very gentle.

My favourite animal is a small grey cat with only one eye. The workers at the shelter call her Lucky. When she first came to the shelter, she was very thin and afraid of people. Now she sits on my lap and sleeps while I read a book to her.

Many people visit the shelter to adopt an animal. When an animal finds a new family, the workers ring a bell in the office. Everybody claps, and sometimes I cry a little because I am happy and sad at the same time.

Last month, a young couple adopted Lucky. I was sad to say goodbye, but I know she has a good home now. The couple sent a photo of her sleeping on their sofa. Volunteering at the shelter is hard work, but it is the most important thing I do every week.

=== easy-long-07 | A Day Without a Phone
Our teacher gave us an unusual homework assignment. We had to spend a whole day without a phone, and then write about it. At first, I thought it would be easy. I was wrong.

On Saturday morning, I gave my phone to my mother. She put it in a drawer in the kitchen. Without it, I didn't know what time it was, so I looked for an old clock in the living room.

After breakfast, I wanted to check my messages. I walked to the kitchen three times before I remembered that my phone was in the drawer. I felt a little nervous. What if my friends were talking about something important?

At eleven o'clock, I was bored, so I went for a walk in the park. Usually I listen to music when I walk, but this time I listened to the birds and the wind in the trees. I saw an old man feeding pigeons and a family having a picnic on the grass. I never noticed these things before.

In the afternoon, I went to my friend's house. I didn't send a message first; I just knocked on the door. His mother was surprised, but she invited me in. We played a board game and talked for a long time. It was more fun than chatting online.

In the evening, I read a book for two hours. I finished the whole book, and I can't remember the last time I did that.

At the end of the day, my mother gave the phone back to me. I had forty messages, but none of them were important. In my homework, I wrote that a day without a phone was difficult, but it was also a nice change. I want to do it again once a month.

=== easy-long-08 | My Brother's Wedding
Last month, my older brother got married. He is twenty-nine, and his wife, Jiyoung, is a nurse at a hospital in our city. They met at a friend's party three years ago.

The wedding was on a sunny Saturday in May. It was in a hotel in the centre of the city. In the morning, the whole family was very busy. My mother went to a salon to do her hair, and my father wore a new suit. I wore a dress that my aunt made for me.

The ceremony started at noon. My brother stood at the front of the hall in a black suit. He looked very nervous. Then the music started, and Jiyoung walked in with her father. She wore a long white dress, and everyone said she looked beautiful.

During the ceremony, my brother read a letter to Jiyoung. In the letter, he wrote about the first time they met. He said he spilled a glass of juice on her shoes. Everybody laughed, and Jiyoung laughed too.

After the ceremony, there was a big lunch for all the guests. There were more than two hundred people. We ate beef, noodles, and a lot of different salads. Then there was a traditional ceremony called pyebaek. My brother and Jiyoung wore hanbok and bowed to our parents and grandparents. My grandmother threw dates and chestnuts at them, and they tried to catch them in a cloth.

In the evening, the couple left for their honeymoon in Vietnam. The house is very quiet without my brother now. I miss him, but I am happy that he has a wonderful wife.

=== easy-long-09 | The School Trip to the Museum
In November, our class went on a trip to the National Museum of Korea in Seoul. We left school at eight o'clock in the morning and travelled by bus. The trip took about an hour and a half.

The museum is a very large building with a beautiful garden in front of it. When we arrived, a guide met us at the entrance. She was a young woman with a soft voice. She gave each of us a small notebook and a pencil.

First, the guide took us to a room with very old things. There were stone tools and clay pots that were thousands of years old. The guide told us that people used the tools to cut meat and the pots to store food. It was hard to believe that people made them so long ago.

Next, we went to a room full of gold. There was a gold crown from the Silla Kingdom. It had many small pieces of jade on it, and it shone under the lights. My friend said it looked like a crown from a movie. The guide explained that only kings and queens wore crowns like this.

After lunch, we had some free time. I visited a room with paintings and a quiet, dark room with two bronze statues of a thinking figure. I sat on a bench and looked at the statues for a long time. It made me feel calm.

At the end of the visit, our teacher asked us to write about the most interesting thing we saw. I wrote about the gold crown. On the bus home, I looked at the pictures in my notebook. It was an interesting day, and I learned a lot about the history of my country.

=== easy-long-10 | Learning to Cook
Until last year, I couldn't cook anything except instant noodles. When my mother started a new job, she often came home late, so I decided to learn to cook.

My first teacher was my grandmother. She showed me how to make rice in a pot and how to cut an onion without crying. She also taught me how to make a simple soup with tofu and vegetables. She said, "A good cook tastes the food many times." I still remember that.

At first, I made a lot of mistakes. Once, I put too much salt in the soup, and nobody could eat it. Another time, I forgot about the rice, and it burned. The smoke filled the kitchen, and the fire alarm went off. My little sister thought it was very funny.

After a few weeks, I started to get better. I watched videos online and tried a new recipe every weekend. I learned to make fried rice, an omelette, and a spicy stew with pork. My favourite dish was a pasta with tomato sauce. The recipe was from an Italian chef, and it was easier than I expected.

Now I cook dinner for my family three times a week. My mother is very happy because she can rest when she comes home. My father always asks for a second bowl, and my sister helps me wash the dishes.

Cooking is now one of my hobbies. I like going to the market and choosing fresh vegetables. I also like seeing my family enjoy the food that I make. Next year, I want to take a cooking class at a community centre. Maybe one day I will be a chef.
